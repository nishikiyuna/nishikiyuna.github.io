Research

  • Lattice QCD analysis of the inter-quark potential
  • Origin of quark confinement seen through gluonic energy components
  • Anomalies and index theorems in quantum field theory

The static potential V(r) between a quark and an antiquark is Coulombic at short distances and grows linearly at large distances. This linear term is the most direct manifestation of quark confinement.

V(r) = −A / r + σ r + C

Which part of the gluon field is responsible for that linear term? We decompose the gluonic field energy into its components in lattice QCD simulations and quantitatively isolate the contributions that build up confinement.
